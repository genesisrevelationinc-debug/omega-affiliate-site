## What are Shopify Pricing Plans?

Shopify offers four main pricing plans: Basic Shopify, Shopify, Shopify Advanced, and Lite. Each plan has its unique set of features, designed to cater to different business sizes and needs. The good news is that Shopify offers a 14-day free trial for all new users, allowing you to test the platform before committing to a plan.

### Basic Shopify Plan

The Basic Shopify plan starts at $29 per month (billed annually). This plan includes:

* Online store with up to 2 staff accounts
* 24/7 support via email and phone
* Payment processing fee of 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction
* Product variants, gift cards, and online invoices

The Basic Shopify plan is ideal for small businesses or individuals just starting out with their online store.

## Pros:

* Affordable pricing
* Suitable for small businesses or solo entrepreneurs
* Essential features for a basic online store

## Cons:

* Limited staff accounts (2)
* 24/7 support is via email and phone only
* Higher payment processing fees compared to other plans

### Shopify Plan

The Shopify plan starts at $79 per month (billed annually). This plan includes all the features of the Basic Shopify plan, plus:

* Online store with up to 5 staff accounts
* 24/7 support via email, phone, and live chat
* Payment processing fee of 2.6% + 30¢ per transaction
* Gift cards, online invoices, and product variants

The Shopify plan is suitable for growing businesses or those that need more features.

## Pros:

* More staff accounts (5) compared to the Basic Shopify plan
* Live chat support available
* Lower payment processing fees compared to the Basic Shopify plan

## Cons:

* Higher monthly pricing compared to the Basic Shopify plan
* Limited online store with only 1 theme included

### Shopify Advanced Plan

The Shopify Advanced plan starts at $299 per month (billed annually). This plan includes all the features of the Shopify plan, plus:

* Online store with up to 15 staff accounts
* 24/7 support via email, phone, live chat, and priority access
* Payment processing fee of 2.4% + 30¢ per transaction
* Gift cards, online invoices, product variants, and online store

The Shopify Advanced plan is ideal for large businesses or those that need a high level of customization.

## Pros:

* High staff accounts (15) compared to other plans
* Priority access to support team
* Customizable checkout experience

## Cons:

* Highest monthly pricing among all plans
* Limited free trials available

### Lite Plan

The Shopify Lite plan starts at $9 per month (billed annually). This plan includes:

* Sell products on Facebook and Instagram with a buy button
* 24/7 support via email and phone
* Payment processing fee of 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction

The Shopify Lite plan is suitable for individuals who want to sell products outside their online store.

## Pros:

* Affordable pricing
* Sell products on Facebook and Instagram
* Essential features for a basic online store

## Cons:

* Limited staff accounts (1)
* No free trials available

## Choosing the Right Shopify Plan

When choosing a Shopify plan, consider your business needs and growth potential. If you're just starting out or have a small online store, the Basic Shopify plan may be sufficient. However, if you need more features or staff accounts, opt for the Shopify or Shopify Advanced plans.
