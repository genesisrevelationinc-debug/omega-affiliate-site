## What is a Crypto Exchange?

A crypto exchange is an online platform that enables users to buy, sell, and trade cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and others. These platforms act as intermediaries between buyers and sellers, facilitating transactions and providing a secure environment for trading.
