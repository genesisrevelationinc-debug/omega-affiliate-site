**What is a VPN?**

Before diving into the specifics, let's cover the basics. A VPN creates a secure, encrypted connection between your device and a VPN server, masking your IP address and location. This allows you to bypass geo-restrictions and access content not available in your region, making it an ideal solution for streaming Netflix.

**Why Do I Need a VPN for Streaming Netflix?**

While Netflix is available in over 190 countries worldwide, its content library varies greatly depending on the region. By using a VPN, you can:

* Access geo-restricted content: Watch movies and TV shows not available in your country or region.
* Bypass ISP throttling: Some internet service providers (ISPs) throttle streaming speeds to prevent congestion. A VPN helps evade this throttling.
* Protect your online identity: Mask your IP address and location, ensuring anonymity while streaming.
