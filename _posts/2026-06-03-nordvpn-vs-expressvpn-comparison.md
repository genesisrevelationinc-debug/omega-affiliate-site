## Security: The Stronghold of Protection

Security is undoubtedly one of the most critical aspects of any VPN service. Both NordVPN and ExpressVPN boast robust security protocols, including AES-256 encryption, which is widely regarded as unbreakable.

However, what sets these services apart from their competitors is their commitment to transparency. For instance, both NordVPN and ExpressVPN maintain a strict no-logging policy, ensuring that your online activities remain private and secure.

### What Does "No-Logging" Mean?

In simple terms, a VPN service with a no-logging policy ensures that it doesn't collect or store any sensitive information about its users. This means that even if you're using a public Wi-Fi network, your VPN provider won't be able to track your online activities.
