# Shopify Pricing Plans Explained

As you're considering building or upgrading an online store, choosing a reliable e-commerce platform is crucial. With numerous options available, selecting the right one can be overwhelming. This article will help you navigate Shopify pricing plans, exploring their features and costs to ensure you find the perfect fit for your business.

## What is Shopify?

Shopify is a popular cloud-based e-commerce platform that allows users to create an online store without extensive technical knowledge. Founded in 2004, it has grown into one of the most widely used platforms globally, with over 2 million active stores. [Visit Shopify's official website](https://shopify.com) for more information.

## Shopify Pricing Plans

Shopify offers four main pricing plans: Basic Shopify, Shopify, and Advanced Shopify. These plans cater to different business needs and sizes. Here's a breakdown of each plan:

### Basic Shopify

* **Cost:** $29 per month
* **Features:**
	+ Sell an unlimited number of products
	+ Use 2 staff accounts
	+ Accept credit cards through Shopify Payments
	+ Enjoy basic reporting and analytics
	+ Get access to online payment security (PCI-DSS compliance)

The Basic Shopify plan is ideal for small, new businesses or those with limited budgets. It provides the necessary tools for a simple online store.

### Shopify

* **Cost:** $79 per month
* **Features:**
	+ All features from the Basic Shopify plan
	+ Use up to 5 staff accounts
	+ Accept credit cards through Shopify Payments
	+ Enjoy advanced reporting and analytics
	+ Get access to online payment security (PCI-DSS compliance)
	+ Use gift cards and discount codes

The Shopify plan is suitable for growing businesses or those requiring more features. It offers additional staff accounts, advanced analytics, and gift card support.

### Advanced Shopify

* **Cost:** $299 per month
* **Features:**
	+ All features from the Shopify plan
	+ Use up to 15 staff accounts
	+ Accept credit cards through Shopify Payments
	+ Enjoy advanced reporting and analytics
	+ Get access to online payment security (PCI-DSS compliance)
	+ Use gift cards and discount codes
	+ Enjoy API access

The Advanced Shopify plan is designed for large businesses or those with complex e-commerce requirements. It offers extensive features, including API access.

## Additional Fees and Charges

When choosing a Shopify pricing plan, it's essential to consider additional fees:

* **Transaction fee:** 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction
* **Payment gateway fees:** Vary depending on the payment method used
* **Shipping rates:** Depend on your store's location and shipping carriers

To minimize these extra costs, consider using Shopify Payments as your primary payment gateway.
