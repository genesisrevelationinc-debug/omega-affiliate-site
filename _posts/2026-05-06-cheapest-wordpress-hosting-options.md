## What to Look for in a Cheap WordPress Host

Before we dive into our top picks, it's essential to know what makes a cheap host worth considering. Here are some key factors to keep an eye out for:

*   **Uptime and Speed**: A reliable host should ensure your website is always accessible to visitors.
*   **Security**: Look for features like free SSL certificates, automatic backups, and malware scanning.
*   **Scalability**: Choose a plan that allows you to upgrade or downgrade as your website grows.
*   **Customer Support**: 24/7 support is a must-have in case you encounter any issues.
