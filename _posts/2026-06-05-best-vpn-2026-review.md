### What is a VPN?

Before diving into our top picks, let's briefly explain what a VPN is and why you need one:

A VPN creates a secure, encrypted tunnel between your device and the internet. This ensures that all online activities are protected from prying eyes, including hackers, ISPs, and even governments. With a VPN, you can browse the web anonymously, access geo-restricted content, and enjoy enhanced security for online transactions.
