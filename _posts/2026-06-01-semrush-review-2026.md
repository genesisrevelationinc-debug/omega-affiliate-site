### What is Semrush?

Launched in 2008 by Oleg Shchegolev and Igor Serebryakov, Semrush has grown to become a leading provider of digital marketing solutions. The platform offers a wide range of tools designed to help businesses improve their online visibility, drive more traffic, and increase conversions.

### Key Features

1.  **Keyword Research**: Semrush's keyword research tool allows you to identify profitable keywords, analyze competition, and track rankings.
2.  **Backlink Analysis**: This feature enables you to monitor your backlinks, identify broken links, and build high-quality backlinks.
3.  **Technical SEO Audit**: The platform provides a comprehensive audit of your website's technical performance, highlighting areas for improvement.
4.  **Content Analysis**: Semrush offers in-depth content analysis, including keyword density, readability scores, and more.
5.  **Competitor Analysis**: This feature helps you understand your competitors' strengths and weaknesses, allowing you to develop effective strategies.

### Pricing Plans

Semrush offers four pricing plans:

1.  **Pro**: $119.95/month (billed annually) - ideal for small businesses or individuals
2.  **Guru**: $229.95/month (billed annually) - suitable for larger businesses or teams
3.  **Business**: $449.95/month (billed annually) - designed for enterprises with complex SEO needs
