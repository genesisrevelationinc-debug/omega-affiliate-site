## What is a VPN?

Before we dive into the specifics of each service, let's quickly cover what a VPN does. A VPN creates a secure, encrypted tunnel between your device and the internet, masking your IP address and protecting your online activity from prying eyes. This is particularly important for those who value their online privacy or engage in activities that require anonymity.
