**What is a Crypto Exchange?**

A cryptocurrency exchange is an online platform that enables users to buy, sell, or trade cryptocurrencies for other digital assets or fiat currencies. These exchanges act as intermediaries between buyers and sellers, providing a secure and efficient way to conduct transactions. Some popular types of crypto exchanges include:

*   **Spot exchanges**: Allow users to buy and sell cryptocurrencies at the current market price.
*   **Derivatives exchanges**: Offer trading options for derivatives such as futures contracts, options, and margin trading.
*   **Fiat-to-crypto exchanges**: Enable users to exchange fiat currencies (e.g., USD) for cryptocurrencies.
