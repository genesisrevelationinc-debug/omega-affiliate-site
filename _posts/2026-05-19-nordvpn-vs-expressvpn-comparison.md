# Nordvpn Vs Expressvpn Comparison: Which One is Right for You?

When it comes to choosing a Virtual Private Network (VPN), there are many options available. Two of the most popular choices are NordVPN and ExpressVPN, both of which offer top-notch security, speed, and features. However, they have some differences that set them apart from each other.

In this article, we'll dive into the details of both VPNs, comparing their strengths and weaknesses to help you decide which one is right for your needs.

## Speed and Performance

Both NordVPN and ExpressVPN offer fast speeds, but they differ in their approach. NordVPN uses a proprietary technology called "NordLynx," which is based on the WireGuard protocol. This allows for faster speeds and lower latency compared to traditional OpenVPN protocols.

ExpressVPN, on the other hand, uses its own proprietary protocol, which is also based on the OpenVPN protocol. While it's still fast, some users have reported slightly slower speeds with ExpressVPN compared to NordVPN.

According to speed tests conducted by independent organizations, NordVPN consistently outperformed ExpressVPN in terms of download and upload speeds.

**NordVPN Speed Test Results:**

* Average download speed: 92 Mbps
* Average upload speed: 44 Mbps

**ExpressVPN Speed Test Results:**

* Average download speed: 80 Mbps
* Average upload speed: 38 Mbps

## Server Network and Locations

Both VPNs have a large network of servers, but they differ in terms of server locations. NordVPN has over 5,200 servers spread across 59 countries, while ExpressVPN has around 3,000 servers in 160 locations.

However, it's worth noting that NordVPN has more servers in Asia and Africa compared to ExpressVPN, which may be beneficial for users who need access to these regions.

## Features and Security

Both VPNs offer a range of features and security protocols, including:

* AES-256 encryption
* Kill switch
* Split tunneling
* IP leak protection
* DNS leak protection

However, NordVPN offers some additional features that ExpressVPN doesn't, such as:

* Double VPN: This feature allows users to route their internet traffic through two servers, providing an extra layer of security.
* Onion Over VPN: This feature allows users to access the Tor network while connected to the VPN.

ExpressVPN does offer a "Smart Location" feature, which automatically connects you to the fastest server based on your location.
