**What is Bitcoin?**

Before we dive into the process of buying Bitcoin, let's cover some basic information about what it is. Bitcoin (BTC) is a decentralized digital currency that allows for peer-to-peer transactions without the need for intermediaries like banks. It was created in 2009 by an individual or group using the pseudonym Satoshi Nakamoto and has since become one of the most widely traded cryptocurrencies.
