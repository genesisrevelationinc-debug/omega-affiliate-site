To ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and prevent money laundering, exchanges require users to verify their identity. This process typically involves:

* **Providing government-issued ID**: Users must submit a valid government-issued ID, such as a driver's license or passport.
* **Proof of address**: Coinbase may request proof of address, such as a utility bill or bank statement.
