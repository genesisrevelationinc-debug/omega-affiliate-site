## Key Features Comparison

Let's break down some of the key features of each tool:

### **Keyword Research**

*   **Semrush**: Offers advanced keyword research tools, including a database of 18 billion keywords and phrases.
*   **Ahrefs**: Provides a keyword explorer with filters for relevance, competition, and search volume.

### **Backlink Analysis**

*   **Semrush**: Includes a backlink audit tool that analyzes your website's link profile and provides recommendations for improvement.
*   **Ahrefs**: Offers a comprehensive backlink database of over 12 trillion links, allowing you to track and analyze your competitors' backlinks.

### **Technical SEO**

*   **Semrush**: Provides a suite of technical SEO tools, including a crawl error report, page speed analysis, and XML sitemap generator.
*   **Ahrefs**: Offers a more limited set of technical SEO features, but includes a website audit tool that identifies areas for improvement.
