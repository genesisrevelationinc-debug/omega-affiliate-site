## What is a Free CRM Software?

Before diving into our list of top picks, let's quickly define what a free CRM software is. A free CRM software is a tool that allows you to manage customer interactions, sales, and marketing efforts without any upfront costs. These tools usually offer basic features such as contact management, lead tracking, and reporting.
