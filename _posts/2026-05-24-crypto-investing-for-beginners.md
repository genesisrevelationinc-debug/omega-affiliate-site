## What is Cryptocurrency?

Before diving into the world of crypto investing, let's first understand what cryptocurrency is. Cryptocurrency is a digital or virtual currency that uses cryptography for security and is decentralized, meaning it's not controlled by any government or financial institution. The most well-known cryptocurrency is Bitcoin, but there are over 5,000 other cryptocurrencies available.

## Types of Cryptocurrencies

There are two main types of cryptocurrencies: altcoins (alternative coins) and tokens. Altcoins, such as Ethereum and Litecoin, have their own blockchain network and can be mined or purchased. Tokens, on the other hand, run on top of another blockchain network, such as Ethereum, and are often used for a specific purpose.

## Blockchain Technology

Blockchain technology is at the heart of cryptocurrency. It's a decentralized ledger that records all transactions made with a particular cryptocurrency. This allows for secure, transparent, and tamper-proof transactions to take place without the need for intermediaries like banks.

### How Does Blockchain Work?

Here's a simplified explanation of how blockchain works:

1. A network of computers is created to validate and record transactions.
2. Each computer on the network has a copy of the entire blockchain.
3. When a new transaction is made, it's broadcast to the network.
4. The network verifies the transaction using complex algorithms.
5. Once verified, the transaction is combined with other transactions in a batch called a block.
6. The block is added to the blockchain, and each computer updates their copy.
