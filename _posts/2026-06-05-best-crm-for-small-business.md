# Best CRM for Small Business: Top Solutions to Streamline Your Sales Process

As a small business owner, managing your sales process can be overwhelming. You have to keep track of leads, customers, and interactions across multiple channels. A good Customer Relationship Management (CRM) software can help you simplify this task and focus on growing your business.

In this article, we'll explore the best CRM for small businesses, including their features, pricing, and reviews. We'll also provide a comparison table to help you choose the right solution for your needs.

## What is a CRM?

Before we dive into the best CRMs for small businesses, let's quickly define what a CRM is. A CRM is software that helps businesses manage customer interactions and data across multiple channels. It typically includes features such as:

* Contact management: storing and organizing customer information
* Sales tracking: monitoring sales activities and performance
* Marketing automation: automating marketing campaigns and workflows
* Customer service: managing support tickets and resolving issues
