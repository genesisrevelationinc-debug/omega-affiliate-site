**What is a CRM?**

Before we dive into the top CRMs, let's quickly define what a CRM is. A Customer Relationship Management system is a software tool designed to manage all interactions with customers, prospects, and partners throughout their entire lifecycle. It helps businesses organize contact information, track communication history, and analyze sales performance.
