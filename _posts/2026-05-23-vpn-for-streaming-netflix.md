**Why Do I Need a VPN for Streaming Netflix?**

Before we dive into the best VPNs for Netflix, let's understand why you need one in the first place. A VPN helps protect your online identity by encrypting your internet traffic, making it virtually impossible for hackers to intercept sensitive information. When streaming Netflix, a VPN ensures that:

1.  Your location is masked, allowing access to geo-restricted content.
2.  You can bypass ISP throttling and buffering issues.
3.  Your data remains secure and private.

**Top Features to Look for in a VPN**

Not all VPNs are created equal. To find the best VPN for streaming Netflix, consider the following features:

1.  **Server locations**: A wide range of server locations ensures you can access content from around the world.
2.  **Speed**: A fast VPN is crucial for seamless streaming experiences.
3.  **Security**: Look for advanced encryption protocols and a no-logs policy to ensure your data remains secure.
4.  **Compatibility**: Ensure the VPN supports your device, including smartphones, laptops, and smart TVs.
