# Shopify Pricing Plans Explained: A Comprehensive Guide

As an e-commerce business owner, choosing the right platform is crucial for success. Shopify is one of the most popular options, but with multiple pricing plans, it can be overwhelming to decide which one suits your needs. In this article, we'll break down each plan, features, and fees to help you make an informed decision.

## Getting Started: Understanding Shopify Pricing

Shopify offers three main pricing plans: Basic Shopify, Shopify, and Advanced Shopify. Each plan has its unique features, and the cost varies depending on the level of customization and support you require.

### Basic Shopify Plan
The Basic Shopify plan is perfect for new businesses or those with a small number of products (up to 2,000). For $29/month plus a 2.9% + 30¢ payment fee per transaction, you get:

* Online store setup
* 24/7 support
* Basic customization options
* Credit card rates from 2.9% + 30¢

[**Get Started with Shopify's Basic Plan**](https://shopify.com/pricing?section=pricing)

## Mid-Range Plans: Shopify and Professional

The Shopify plan is ideal for growing businesses or those that need more features, such as product variations (up to 100) and gift cards. For $79/month plus a payment fee of 2.6% + 30¢ per transaction, you get:

* Everything in the Basic Shopify plan
* Product options (e.g., size, color)
* Gift cards
* API access

[**Sign up for Shopify's Standard Plan**](https://shopify.pxf.io/standard)

The Professional plan is suitable for larger businesses or those requiring advanced features like abandoned cart recovery and real-time carrier shipping. For $299/month plus a payment fee of 2.4% + 30¢ per transaction, you get:

* Everything in the Shopify plan
* Abandoned cart recovery
* Real-time carrier shipping rates
* API access

## Advanced Plans: Advanced Shopify and Online Store 2.0

The Advanced Shopify plan is designed for large businesses or those requiring advanced features like multiple staff accounts and API access. For $299/month plus a payment fee of 2% + 30¢ per transaction, you get:

* Everything in the Professional plan
* Multiple staff accounts
* API access
* Custom reporting

[**Get Started with Advanced Shopify**](https://shopify.com/pricing?section=pricing)

## Online Store 2.0: The Future of E-commerce

Shopify's latest offering is Online Store 2.0, a more modern and customizable platform for large businesses or those requiring advanced features like custom themes and liquid code editing. For $2,000/month plus a payment fee of 1% + 30¢ per transaction, you get:

* Everything in the Advanced Shopify plan
* Custom themes
* Liquid code editing
* API access

## Choosing the Right Plan for Your Business

When selecting a pricing plan, consider your business's growth stage and requirements. If you're just starting out, the Basic Shopify plan is perfect. For growing businesses or those requiring more features, opt for the Shopify or Professional plans.
