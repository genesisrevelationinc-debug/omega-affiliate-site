## Understanding SEO Basics

Before diving into the world of SEO tools, it's essential to understand the basics. Search engines like Google use algorithms to rank websites based on relevance and authority. This means that a website with high-quality content, a strong domain authority, and relevant keywords will outrank one without these factors.

To improve your website's visibility, you'll need to focus on three core areas:

1. **Keyword Research**: Identifying the most relevant and high-traffic keywords for your business.
2. **On-page Optimization**: Ensuring your website is optimized with meta tags, header tags, and internal linking.
3. **Off-page Optimization**: Building backlinks from authoritative sources to increase domain authority.

### 3. Moz: SEO Audit and Keyword Research

Moz is a popular tool for SEO audits and keyword research. With Moz, you can:

**Pros:** Robust SEO audit capabilities, comprehensive keyword database, user-friendly interface.
