**Why Do You Need a VPN for Remote Work?**

Before we dive into our top picks, let's quickly discuss why a VPN is essential for remote workers. A VPN creates a secure, encrypted connection between your device and the internet, protecting your data from prying eyes. This is particularly important when working remotely, as public Wi-Fi networks can be notoriously insecure.

**Key Features to Look for in a VPN**

When selecting a VPN for remote work, consider the following essential features:

* **Strong encryption**: Ensure the VPN uses industry-standard AES-256 encryption or better.
* **Server network**: Opt for a VPN with a large server network to minimize latency and ensure reliable connectivity.
* **Speed**: Choose a VPN that won't slow down your internet connection.
* **Security protocols**: Look for a VPN that supports multiple security protocols, such as OpenVPN, L2TP/IPSec, and PPTP.
