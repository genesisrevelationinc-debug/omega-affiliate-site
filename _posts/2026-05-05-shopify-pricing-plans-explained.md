# Shopify Pricing Plans Explained: Choosing the Right Plan for Your Business

With over 1 million active merchants, Shopify has become one of the most popular e-commerce platforms. One of the key reasons behind its success is its flexibility and scalability. Shopify offers a range of pricing plans to suit different business needs, from small online stores to large enterprises.

In this article, we'll break down the various Shopify pricing plans, their features, costs, and pros/cons. By the end of this guide, you'll be able to choose the right plan for your business.

## Basic Shopify Plan

The Basic Shopify plan is ideal for new businesses or small online stores with a limited product range. The starting price is $29 per month, which includes:

* Online store
* 2 staff accounts
* 24/7 support
* Payment processing fees (2.9% + 30¢ per transaction)
* Shipping rates

However, the Basic Shopify plan has some limitations, including:

* Limited storage space (1 GB)
* Limited bandwidth (5 GB)
* No gift cards or abandoned cart recovery
* Limited API access

If you expect high sales volumes or need more advanced features, consider upgrading to a higher plan.

## Shopify Plan

The Shopify plan is the most popular and versatile option. Starting at $79 per month, it offers:

* Online store
* 5 staff accounts
* 24/7 support
* Payment processing fees (2.9% + 30¢ per transaction)
* Shipping rates
* Gift cards and abandoned cart recovery
* API access

Compared to the Basic Shopify plan, this option provides more storage space (10 GB), bandwidth (50 GB), and staff accounts.

## Advanced Shopify Plan

The Advanced Shopify plan is designed for larger businesses or those with complex e-commerce needs. The starting price is $299 per month, which includes:

* Online store
* 15 staff accounts
* 24/7 support
* Payment processing fees (2.9% + 30¢ per transaction)
* Shipping rates
* Gift cards and abandoned cart recovery
* API access
* Advanced report building

This plan also offers more storage space (10 GB), bandwidth (600 GB), and API calls.

## Shopify Plus Plan

Shopify Plus is a premium plan designed for large enterprises or businesses with custom e-commerce requirements. The price varies depending on the business needs, but expect to pay around $2,000 per month.

This plan includes:

* Custom storefronts
* Advanced report building
* API access
* 24/7 support
* Payment processing fees (2.9% + 30¢ per transaction)
* Shipping rates

Shopify Plus offers more storage space, bandwidth, and staff accounts compared to the Advanced Shopify plan.

## Choosing the Right Plan for Your Business

When choosing a Shopify pricing plan, consider your business needs and goals. Ask yourself:

* Do you need advanced features or custom storefronts?
* How many products do you expect to sell per month?
* Do you require more storage space or bandwidth?

If you're unsure about which plan is right for your business, consider the following:

* Start with the Basic Shopify plan and upgrade as needed.
* Take advantage of Shopify's 14-day free trial to test the waters.

## Pros and Cons of Each Plan

Here's a summary of the pros and cons of each Shopify pricing plan:

### Basic Shopify

Pros: Affordable, easy to use
