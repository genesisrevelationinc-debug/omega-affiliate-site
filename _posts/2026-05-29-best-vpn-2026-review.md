## What is a VPN?

Before diving into the best VPNs for 2026, it's essential to understand what a VPN is and how it works. A VPN creates a secure, encrypted connection between your device and a server, masking your IP address and encrypting your internet traffic. This prevents hackers, ISPs, and even government agencies from tracking your online activities.

## Key Features of the Best VPNs

When selecting a VPN provider, consider the following essential features:

1.  **Server locations**: A large network of servers in various countries ensures faster speeds and better access to geo-restricted content.
2.  **Encryption protocols**: Look for providers using robust encryption protocols like AES-256-GCM or IKEv2/IPSec.
3.  **Speed**: Choose a provider with fast speeds, low latency, and minimal packet loss.
4.  **Security features**: Ensure the VPN offers features like kill switch, DNS leak protection, and split tunneling.
5.  **User interface**: Opt for a provider with an intuitive user interface that's easy to navigate.
