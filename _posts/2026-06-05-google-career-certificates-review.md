Each certificate is built around three core principles:

1.  **Expert Instruction**: Google Career Certificates are developed in collaboration with industry experts, ensuring that the content is relevant and up-to-date.
2.  **Hands-on Projects**: The program includes real-world projects that allow learners to apply their skills practically.
3.  **Industry-Recognized**: Upon completion, individuals receive a certificate that is recognized by employers worldwide.
