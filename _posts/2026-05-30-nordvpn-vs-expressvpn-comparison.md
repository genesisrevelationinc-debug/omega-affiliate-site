## What is a VPN?

Before we dive into the comparison, let's quickly explain what a VPN is. A VPN creates a secure and encrypted connection between your device and a VPN server, masking your IP address and encrypting internet traffic. This ensures that your online activities remain private and protected from hacking, surveillance, and other cyber threats.
