**What is a VPN?**

A VPN is a virtual network that encrypts internet traffic between your device and the VPN server, making it virtually impossible for hackers to intercept or monitor your online activities. By using a VPN, you can:

* Secure public Wi-Fi connections
* Bypass geo-restrictions and access blocked content
* Protect your identity and data from cyber threats
