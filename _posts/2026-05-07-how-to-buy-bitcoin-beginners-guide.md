**Step 1: Understand the Basics**

Before you start buying Bitcoin, it's essential to understand the basics. Bitcoin is a decentralized digital currency that uses cryptography for secure financial transactions. It was created by an anonymous individual or group of individuals using the pseudonym Satoshi Nakamoto in 2009.

Bitcoin is not issued by any central authority and has a limited supply of 21 million coins. The blockchain technology behind Bitcoin allows for fast, secure, and transparent transactions.
